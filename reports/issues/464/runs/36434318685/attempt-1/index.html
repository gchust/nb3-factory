问题 01 · verification

ESLint unbound-method error in materials seed

本任务已处理

eslint . --max-warnings 0 failed with `@typescript-eslint/unbound-method` at database/main/seeds/202612010002_materials_seed.ts:31:15, so the lint stage exited 1.

原因（实现者自述）
`async run({ repository })` destructured the `repository` factory method off SeedContext and then invoked it as a free function, detaching it from its receiver; the rule cannot verify `this` is unused and rejects the call.
处理方式
Changed the seed to `async run(context)` and call `context.repository('materials')`, keeping the method bound to its object. Scoped eslint on the file and `tsc -p tsconfig.server.json --noEmit` both pass.
影响 / 代价：未提供，不估算来源：Agent · retro.json
